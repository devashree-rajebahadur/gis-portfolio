Assignment reflections

GIS Mapping · Field Data Collection

Automated buffer analysis

This assignment explored the spatial distribution of hospitals, clinics and pharmacies in Erandwane, Pune. Healthcare facilities in Erandwane exhibit a clustered pattern. Pharmacies and clinics are frequently located near major roads, particularly Karve Road and Gulwani Maharaj Road. Their proximity suggests complementary services, while gaps in coverage may reflect variations in population density, land use, and commercial development.

Doing this short mapping exercise made me appreciate the attention to detail required when designing a data collection schema, and the amount of effort needed in collecting ground data. While building the Epicollect5 form, I made sure to include all possible attributes in the form of questions and structure the form in such a way that later data handling would be convenient. While collecting the data, I realized that a great variety of clinics — physiotherapy, sonography, gynecology, and laser to name a few — are present in my neighborhood, which is something I had never previously noticed.

One of the mistakes I made while doing this assignment was that I collected the data at night, and therefore could not take photos of the recorded features.

Overall Reflection

Developing a spatial perspective on real-world problems.

From Maps to Meaningful Analysis

This course helped me understand that geospatial science involves much more than creating maps. It requires an understanding of how geographic data is collected, structured, referenced, processed and interpreted. Each stage influences the reliability of the final output.

As a geology student interested in geoinformatics, I see these fundamentals as an important foundation for further work with GIS and remote sensing. Whether studying landscape change, mapping environmental features or analysing spatial relationships, I need to consider data quality, coordinate systems, suitable analytical methods and the way results are communicated.

Moving forward, I hope to strengthen these skills through practical projects that use geospatial technologies to investigate environmental and resource-management questions. I want to develop the ability to move from a clearly defined problem to a well-supported spatial analysis and an informative final map.
