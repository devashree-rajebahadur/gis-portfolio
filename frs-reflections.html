Remote sensing

Studying Earth from space

Fundamentals of Remote Sensing introduced me to the concepts of remote sensing, specifically how satellite data is captured, processed, analyzed, and applied in various use cases, along with the basics of photogrammetry with airborne platforms such as airplanes and UAVs.

What I Have Learnt

Reflections on the main concepts covered throughout the course.

01

The electromagnetic spectrum

I learnt about the physics behind remote sensing — how electromagnetic radiation propagates through space and its interactions with matter, which is the basis of remote sensing. I understood how different parts of the spectrum are used to study different aspects of Earth, for instance, we use NIR to study vegetation, and microwaves to measure ground deformation.

02

Remote sensing platforms

Learning about the remote sensing platforms — satellites, airplanes, UAVs — helped me understand the different ways in which remote sensing data are collected and the pros and cons of each method. I realised that the selection of a platform is dependent on the purpose of the project. I also learnt about various government and commercial EO programs, their satellite constellations, and their applications.

03

Sensors

Studying the types and mechanisms of remote sensing sensors helped me understand how each sensor, with its spectral bands, swath, spatial resolution, is designed for a specific purpose. Choosing a sensor and platform for a remote sensing project depend entirely on its purpose, and there are always trafe-offs. For instance, one satellite may have a coarse spatial resolution but it has historical imagery archives, and another might have fine resolution but lack older imagery.

04

Photogrammetry

Exploring aerial photography helped me understand how different geographic features can be studied in great detail with method. I learnt about aerial photography with airplanes and UAVs, and the defects that can arise in them due to operational errors as well as natural causes. I also studied the calculation of scale of aerial photographs. This helped me understand the logic behind the automated software process.

07

Applications of remote sensing

Exploring applications in disaster management, mining, urban planning, and environmental studies helped me understand the broad relevance of remote sensing. This strengthened my interest in applying remote sensing to environmental and natural resource management.

Assignment reflections

GIS Mapping · Field Data Collection

Campus Tree Tagging in BVDU

The MSc Geoinformatics-I class started the BVDU Campus Tree Tagging project in the month of September, systematically dividing the campus into 10 zones, and marking and tagging trees to create a dashboard. I contributed to data collection by identifying and geotagging over 180 trees using ArcGIS Survey123. This is an ongoing project and is expected to be completed by the end of the academic year.

Through this assignment, I learned how to use a new tool, Survey123, for geospatial data collection, how to identify some common trees, and how to work in a team.
